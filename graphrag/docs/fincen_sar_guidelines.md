# FinCEN Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Regulatory Guidelines (31 CFR § 1020.320)

## 1. Overview & Statutory Requirements
The Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) requires financial institutions to report suspicious activity regarding potential violations of law or regulation.

## 2. Threshold Triggers
1. **Insider Abuse**: Any amount, known or suspected.
2. **Aggregated Suspicious Transactions $\ge \$5,000$**: Where the institution can identify a suspect or syndicate (e.g. shared device ID, mule ring accounts).
3. **Aggregated Suspicious Transactions $\ge \$25,000$**: Regardless of whether a suspect is identified.
4. **Structuring & Rapid Layering**: Transactions structured to evade BSA reporting thresholds or rapid cross-account dispersal.

## 3. Mandatory 4-Part Narrative Standard
1. **Executive Summary**: Core finding, total dollar amount, primary suspect accounts, and date range.
2. **Entity & Identity Graph**: Graph connections, shared devices, linked cards, proxy accounts, and community clustering findings.
3. **Chronological Transaction Flow**: Date/time breakdown of unauthorized charges, velocity bursts, and transfer attempts.
4. **Law Enforcement & Regulatory Conclusion**: Grounded typology classification, TigerGraph query evidence, and immediate containment actions executed.
